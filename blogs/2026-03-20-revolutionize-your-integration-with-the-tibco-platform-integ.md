<p>In today’s data-driven landscape, generative AI is only as powerful as the context you provide. To move beyond generic responses and leverage your own enterprise data, <strong>Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)</strong> is the gold standard. With the release of the <strong>TIBCO Platform Integration &#8211; BusinessWorks<img alt="™" class="wp-smiley" src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/72x72/2122.png" style="height: 1em;" /> Plug-in for AI</strong>, we are bringing these advanced AI capabilities directly into your existing integration workflows.<br /></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is RAG and Why Does it Matter?</strong></h2>



<p>RAG integrates information retrieval with generative AI models by pulling relevant data from your internal documents, databases, or knowledge repositories. This context is then supplied to the AI model to ensure outputs are accurate, domain-specific, and relevant &#8211; all without the need for expensive model retraining.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Boost Accuracy</strong>: Reduces AI &#8220;hallucinations&#8221; by grounding responses in your data.<br /></li>



<li><strong>Domain Expertise</strong>: Provides up-to-date information specific to your business.<br /></li>



<li><strong>Versatility</strong>: Supports high-value use cases like intelligent question answering, content summarization, and automated decision support.</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Low-Code RAG: Implementing Use Cases in Minutes</strong></h2>



<p>The new <strong>RAG Palette</strong> simplifies complex AI architectures into three intuitive activities that fit right into your BusinessWorks processes:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Chunk Activity</strong>: Automatically breaks large datasets or files into manageable segments using Paragraph-Based or Semantic strategies.<br /></li>



<li><strong>Ingest Activity</strong>: Generates embedding vectors and stores them in a vector database.<br /></li>



<li><strong>Query Activity</strong>: Sends questions to the AI system to retrieve relevant answers based on your unique ingested data.</li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Fast-Track Your AI Journey</strong></h2>



<p>Getting started is straightforward. The plug-in supports leading LLM services available in the market. Simply configure your <strong>RAG Connection</strong> shared resource, drop the activities onto your canvas, and you’re ready to test.</p>



<p>Whether you are processing travel itineraries or managing complex customer profiles, the TIBCO BusinessWorks<img alt="™" class="wp-smiley" src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/72x72/2122.png" style="height: 1em;" /> Plug-in for AI provides the tools to build sophisticated, AI-enhanced applications with the same reliability and ease you expect from TIBCO.</p>
